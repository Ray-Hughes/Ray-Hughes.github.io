The files here were recompressed with `gltf-transform meshopt` (textures
embedded); geometry and look are unchanged.

The landmark buildings (Hughes Tower, the hospital, the temple, the library,
the cinema, the university, the post office), the cranes, the island and the
water are modelled in code in `src/city` and `src/scenes`.

The sign lettering uses **Lilita One** by Juan Montoreano, under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 (`public/fonts/OFL-LilitaOne.txt`).
